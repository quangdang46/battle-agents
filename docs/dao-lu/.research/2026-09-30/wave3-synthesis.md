"# DAO-LU WAVE 4 — SYNTHESIS\n\nSixteen domain reports, each attacked by a skeptic; a negative audit that calibrated itself against four known disproofs before testing thirteen negatives. Where a skeptic and a researcher disagree, the skeptic won throughout. The premise this wave was convened to test is **false as stated**; the salvage is narrower and considerably more useful.\n\n---\n\n## THE CENTRAL QUESTION\n\n**If a major realm buys permission rather than a bigger number, what resolves a fight?**\n\nThree devices ship. Only three. All three are in the evidence, most are first-party.\n\n**1. A difficulty constant chosen before the fight and applied to the player.** 觅长生's five bands — 极简 (+5 cards/turn, −50% damage taken), 简单 (+2 cards, −20% taken), 普通 (nothing), 困难 (+20% taken, −20% dealt), 极难 (+50% taken, −50% dealt). No monster stat table exists. The encounter is an authored, published, pre-committed constant. This is the **only** device in the corpus that works for solo PvE, and it works *because* the difficulty is legible before the fight is accepted.\n\n**2. A named damage-type bypass.** 觅长生's own 词缀 text: 毒/灼烧/暗劲/剑气 「不是技能伤害，可以无视霜冻层数和天机阁修士的免伤，所以灼烧流也能随意碾压金丹天机阁修士」. 寻道大千's 真实伤害 (six named skills) and 灵兽 ignore 防御 entirely and both skip 增伤/减伤. This is how a lower realm wins with no realm coefficient anywhere. It is the genre's real answer to 境界碾压, and it is load-bearing beyond the studio's control: a 2026 Skymods entry exists **precisely to add 境界压制**, because the base game ships none. The ladder was never the answer; the bypass was, and tuning could not close it.\n\n**3. A counter or a clock as the settlement, never as the damage.** 51神仙道 at 25 rounds by 总伤害/总损血 (accumulated output, not a prospective stat — a fighter behind on HP wins by having done more total damage). 梦镜仙缘's cap tiebreak reads 战力 **inversely**: 「血量相同则总战力低的一方获胜」 — the smaller number wins. 弈仙牌 resolves on 生命值 and 轮次数 only. 梦幻西游手游's 决胜 state from round 51 (+10% damage, reduced healing per round, capped ±50% at round 55). 永夜降临: 「战斗天平会在 8回合后提高全场的伤害，并且降低治疗量」. 4399梦幻修仙's 仙盟战 is 500 点 of 结界体力, −15 per flag, 34 deliveries to win. 修仙家族模拟器2's 驻地攻防 is last-hit on a shared object with a 30-minute cap; 万年兽潮 is object integrity at a timestamp with no death at all.\n\n**The closest shipped precedent to the design, and it is not a fight:** 觅长生's 星河神秘洞府 is a **per-turn output threshold against a fixed HP pool** — 2800 HP, ~70–80 output/turn clears stage 1, 500/turn clears stage 3, a 6.25x curve in required throughput with no multiplier, behind authored enemy realms (金丹中期, then 元婴初期). That is a scalar-free solo PvE **content check**. It is a throughput gate, not a win bit.\n\n**What the genre proved by shipping it, and the design must not copy:** a stat difference inside the damage expression. Roughly fifteen titles run 攻击−防御. None of them can be fought from the panel, and 崩坏:星穹铁道 ships a **hidden** 10% 减伤 on un-broken enemies that 「不会显示在状态列表中」. The panel is not the fight. A design that puts a power number on screen and expects an LLM to compute with it is building a system whose state the model cannot represent.\n\n**The structural concession nobody has shipped:** a fight can be scalar-free; **the loot cannot**, because rarity is an order. Permission the realm buys must be permission to *reach the row*, not to *win* it, and the reward must be keyed to a visible category — 首次通关, 层数, 周常 — not to a difficulty comparison. This has no shipped precedent in the corpus. That is the open slot, and it is open for a real reason.\n\n---\n\n## turnModel\n\n| Structure | Numbers + denominators | Machine cost | Turn-denominated? |\n|---|---|---|---|\n| **梦幻西游 PC** (first-party, 2013-01-10) | 30 s/order; timeout → silent 物理攻击; 150-round cap; disconnect = timeout every round. This is the list to avoid.\n\n1. **Difference-of-aggregates in the damage expression** — 寻道大千, 修仙家族模拟器, 汉家江湖 (floored at 等级系数/3), 无极仙途, 剑与远征, 碧蓝档案. The most common shape in the genre and the least readable.\n2. **Multiplicative realm coefficient** — 一念逍遥 境界碾压, an exponential N× per step, the 主策划's own stated founding framework, with documented costs: head players 碾压 5–10 ordinary players, 滚服 as a direct consequence, 单服 as the forced social unit.\n3. **Power-difference comparison with a published multiplier** — 太吾绘卷 精纯值: **one point of purity advantage = +50% 破体/破气, +20% opponent 护体/御气** (1.25x net swing per point, community-identified as the meta stat). 鬼谷八荒 gates 切磋 on 实力差值, keys 论道 to 境界差 on both sides, and gates 藏匿/索取/双修 on 实力差距.\n4. **Threshold comparisons that zero damage** — 太吾绘卷: any one of 力道/拆招/闪避 exceeding the defender's corresponding attribute sets 技能威力 = **0**. 鬼谷八荒 保护值 = **0** across one realm of gap (else 0.3), plus an absolute rule that a technique two realms higher always crushes.\n5. **Bounded linear difference — the safe instance** — 寻道大千's four instances at 1%/100 points, 50% cap, 30% proc. Copy this.\n6. **Displayed aggregate that is also consumed** — 寻道大千 妖力; 一念逍遥 战力 + 混沌榜; 诛仙2 道法值; 问剑长生 宗门战力; 永夜降临 总战力 leaderboard + pre-match 战力对比; 诛仙 灵气值 leaderboard (official page, 2009); 修仙家族模拟器 实力值 (Σ per disciple, and **the same number as the 灵气 recruitment cost**).\n7. **Matchmaking on a scalar** — 凡人修仙传 brackets PvP by 队伍总战力; 问剑长老 宗门联赛 into 天/地/玄/黄 by 宗门战力; 一念逍遥 宗门对决 matches on closest 战力 *and freezes a 历史最高战力 snapshot specifically to deny power-dropping to dodge a matchup*; 梦幻西游 封神演武 sorts candidates by 等级/段位 proximity.\n8. **Access gating on a scalar** — 修仙家族模拟器 实力值 band caps which diplomat realms may address a target at all; 诛仙2 帮派等级 from 建设度 gates 宣战 eligibility; 远征Online 帮会等级 4–6 from **national** rank; 诛仙手游 帮派活跃度 is a headcount, displayed in the public list.\n9. **Underdog compensation keyed to the gap** — 一念逍遥 宗门对决: **+50% reward above an 8% total-战力 gap, +90% above 15%, combat untouched.** The only place the genre used a power number to *compensate* rather than to sort. Directly transplantable.\n10. **Scalar in the economy** — 问剑长生 sorts resource reclamation by 战力 (「将基于战力由低至高的顺序将多出的法纹自动转化为残纹」). The genre does not separate accumulation from ranking; it does so in one subsystem while ranking in another.\n11. **Scalar one step upstream of the fight** — 最强祖师: 「盟主可以根据盟员战力指配攻打任务」. The leader sorts members by 战力 to assign targets. Resolution is clean; allocation is not.\n12. **Craft-output scalar** — ACS 品阶 (above). The purest algebraic scalar in the corpus.\n13. **Item-gated capacity order** — 鬼谷八荒 rings (炼 cultivator with the right ring out-carries a 筑基 without); 问剑长生 per-realm storage tiers; **一念逍遥 refunded every purchased 灵兽阵 slot in writing** — a studio conceding in public that a slot had become a power axis.\n14. **Char-gen enforcement** — 觅长生 遁术 (initiative threshold, +1/+1/+4 → +8/+8/+32 across the run), 资质 (a rate gated by a second multiplier), 悟性 (5 points ≈ 30 days of study), 连击 (a ×2 shield proc gated on starting with a 双灵根), 体质 (~10x HP separation between two origins), sect access (six gates: 悟性 threshold, element locks, a combat result, an event flag).\n15. **State the player cannot see** — 崩坏 韧性's hidden 10% 减伤, 「不会显示在状态列表中」. The most dangerous class for an LLM population, because the model cannot represent what the UI withholds. 遗水寒's own admission is the warning label: skill 减伤 moved additive→max-of because 「減傷循環不對就暴斃」.\n\n### The inverse list — six shipped ways to break a scalar without breaking the game\n\nThese are more useful than the 15 above, and no report assembled them.\n\n- **Displayed, not consumed.** 修仙家族模拟器2 兽潮 publishes 战力 and states 「并不是只计算一个聚合战力」 — and keys strength to 宗门等级 explicitly, 「不会根据宗门最强成员或宗门当前总战力调整」.\n- **Displayed and declared non-authoritative by the studio.** 一起修仙 官方 FAQ: 「战力是有一定浮动空间的，显示的战斗力只是一个参照不能作为最终判定」.\n- **Formally incomplete, admitted by the studio that sells it.** 御剑红尘: a 天启石 at 2/2 whose second stone adds no 属性 contributes **0 战力** — live in combat, dead in the score.\n- **A scalar you spend, not one you are ranked by.** 寻道大千 千界战场: 「每次挑战后，属性、战力会下降，直至结束挑战」. 妖力 here is a consumable.\n- **A ladder that is not a magnitude.** 诛仙手游 炼器等级 2/9/15 each trigger 外观变化 — a cosmetic ladder parallel to and not derivable from the numeric one.\n- **No aggregate at all.** 修仙小屋: 「玩家直接没有最强，基本都是互相克制」, with leaderboards per attribute *within* a realm band and never across realms.\n\nAdd a seventh, from the negative audit: **clamping rather than ranking** — 寻道大千's 天道制衡 balances a short-matched player 「至接近该组别的程度」. It still reads 妖力 for a +20% bonus, so it is a reduction, not an elimination, but it is the only shipped equaliser.\n\n---\n\n## nonScalarCombat\n\n**It is not empty, and the shape is sharp.** Every instance in the corpus uses exactly one of five devices, and not one uses a power number.\n\n| Device | Instances | Verdict |\n|---|---|---|\n| **Cap + tiebreak on something already committed** | 51神仙道 总伤害/总损血; 梦镜仙缘 剩余总血量 and the **inverse-战力** tiebreak; 弈仙牌 生命值+轮次数; 修仙物语 剩余总血量; 梦幻新诛仙 存活角色数 first, 战斗评分 only as final tiebreak | **Strongest family.** Four first-party sources |\n| **State switch keyed to the clock** | 梦幻西游手游 决胜 (r51→55); 永夜降临 战斗天平 (r8); 凡人修仙传OL 天谴/天诛; 修仙家族模拟器2 驻地攻防 and 万年兽潮 (no death at all) | Applied identically to all combatants; no build interacts |\n| **Constant applied to the player, read from nobody** | 觅长生's five difficulty bands; 修仙模拟器's per-realm AP budget | The only family that reaches solo PvE. It has shipped a scalar-free solo PvE *content check* (觅长生's per-turn output threshold against a fixed pool), which is a throughput gate, not a win bit.\n\n---\n\n## legitimatelyOpen\n\nRanked by what the design would gain, each with the reason nobody took it.\n\n**1. A scalar-free boss fight whose loot is keyed to a visible category rather than a difficulty comparison.** The fight is solved (constant + type bypass + counter settlement). The *reward* is not, because rarity is an order and every engine prints a damage number for free. Nobody took it because the design problem was never legible as a design problem: it reads as \"bosses are hard\" until you notice the tension is between the fight and the drop table, and those were always specified by different people. **This is the slot the wave exists for, and no report found a precedent for it.**\n\n**2. A generic good-enough training representation, built deliberately.** 太吾绘卷's difficulty is an **8-axis vector** (攻击速度/移动速度/命中/破体破气/化解/护体御气内息/施毒量/真气数量, across 4 tiers, plus a separate enemy-count multiplier 1/2/3/4). Because the axes are independent, a 极难 unit at 150% 命中 / 125% 攻速 is **not** strictly stronger than one at 200% 破体破气 / 150% 移速 for a build that never uses 破体. 鬼谷八荒's 兽潮 scores on hit frequency, which is *maximal for a weaker character*. Both are shipped; **neither studio describes either as the point** — one report calls the vector a spreadsheet, the other calls the frequency metric a rate. Nobody took it because each is individually explainable in balance terms, and a designer has to already be thinking about representation learning to notice the two compose. A difficulty instrument that is a vector rather than a scalar is this project's own repo rule, already solved in the genre, and misdescribed as the failure mode.\n\n**3. A versioned, dated formula contract.** 大话西游 has two contradicting official-domain articles — 1.0 floor / +1.4 ceiling (2016) and −60% / +42% (2020). Nobody shipped a stable published formula. **Reason: commercial.** A diffable formula makes balance changes legible as changes, which is an argument, and arguments are bad in a gacha economy.\n\n**4. Publishing the associativity rule inside a defence zone.** 原神 multiplies 减防×无视防御; 崩坏 adds them. Two studios, opposite rules, neither stated. **Reason: technical.** The rule falls out of engine evaluation order, and stating it constrains every future balance patch.\n\n**5. Pure round-index settlement with no HP term.** 觅长生 grades 碾压/大胜/险胜 on round count plus remaining HP — three states, one of them non-binary, and no other title in the corpus has a third state. **Reason: genre expectation.** A health-based grade is visibly better to players and costs nothing to add, so the health term wins every time.\n\n**6.
